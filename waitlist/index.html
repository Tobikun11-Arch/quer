How it works

  1. Plan on the board

    Add post ideas as cards. Give each weekday a theme, or plan a fresh week.

  2. Your Claude edits

    When a card is ready, Queue hands it to Claude Code on your computer to trim, caption and resize.

  3. You approve, Queue posts

    Finished videos wait in Checking. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

Queue is in early development. Requires a desktop computer and Claude Code. Queue is an independent product and is not made by Anthropic.
